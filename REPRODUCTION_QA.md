# Packaging and independent-replay checks

Packaging date: 18 September 2026. The package was assembled from the Round 16 IJDE manuscript pair and frozen 2013–2021 analyses. The ZIP was extracted into a separate fresh directory, and its `run_replication.py --full` command completed all offline statistical stages without reading raw satellite files or project-local study data outside the extracted package. Scientific Python libraries were supplied by the validation runtime; they are not vendored in the ZIP.

The extracted replay returned **PASS** for 25 reproduction-gate rows, rebuilt 4,076 stand-alone product-prefecture-year prediction rows, and found no failures across 32,264 nested benchmark leakage-audit rows. It regenerated 32 annual and 8 pooled conditional comparison cells. The frozen-output comparator checked 59 CSV files (schema, row counts, numeric columns and core categorical identifiers); the largest absolute numeric difference was `3.55271e-15` at the validated runtime. The strict balanced sample contained 272 prefectures. The moderator analysis recorded zero future-information failures. Round 14's 2017 evaluation-fold sensitivity did not change the primary conclusion.

The file inventory and SHA-256 list in `PACKAGE_SHA256.csv` protect packaged inputs, code, provenance and frozen reference outputs. Files labeled `mutable_on_full_run=yes` are expected to change byte-for-byte when scripts regenerate timestamps, local paths and results; their numeric fields are compared with the immutable `reference_results/` copies. The original Round 4 source inventory is excluded from row-for-row comparison because the portable package intentionally omits earlier manuscript and private-prompt entries. This exclusion does not apply to the numerical prediction and leakage gates.

Limitations of this QA: no raw EOG/NASA downloads or new GDP certification were performed in this packaging pass; exact raster-to-city and raw-source-to-panel rebuilding remain outside the offline runner. Map SVGs were copied verbatim from the bound Word files. Third-party GeoJSON is required to redraw map boundaries from scratch. The later Figure 1 revision is a separate candidate asset and remains unintegrated with the bound Word file.
