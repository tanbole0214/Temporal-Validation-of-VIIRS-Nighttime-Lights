# Data access

The publicly bundled files are aggregate statistical results generated by the authors. Row-level GDP values, analysis-ready city panels, prediction rows, full loss draws and the compiled 296-prefecture GDP workbook are not bundled.

The compiled workbook was obtained from Markdata (马克集数; https://www.macrodatas.cn/). Its redistribution permission has not been established. Provider terms govern access to the workbook and any restricted derivatives. No permission to republish it, or unconditional ability to provide it to reviewers, is asserted by this archive.

Additional author-controlled processing metadata and verification documentation may be requested from the corresponding author, Zhong Li (lizhong@caas.cn). Requests for restricted inputs for peer review or research verification must be considered within the applicable provider permissions and legal terms. Detailed city-year verification records and acquired publications are retained outside the public deposit. A request channel is not a guarantee that every third-party file can be supplied.

Seven predeclared verification observations remain unresolved; the verified-enhanced panel retains 298 compiled entries. These qualifications are unchanged by public packaging. The 98 completed sampled verifications must not be conflated with the 108 total new official entries.
